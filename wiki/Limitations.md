# Limitations

### Limitations

Not yet expressible without engine changes: **autocast**; **cooldown reduction / reset**; **hero / creep / building** target masks; **channel ticks** (`on_channel_tick` is defined but the shared pipeline does not emit it — custom mechanics only); delayed effects other than a `zone`'s opening delay; cooldown floors other than explicit level lists; an internal-cooldown gate for reactive passives; aura limits: auras are pulsed with a 0.5 s linger and reach every allied unit (creeps and buildings included; there is no hero mask), slow resistance only applies to slows that land after it, and boots move speed stacks additively (there is no "boots do not stack" rule); applier-side status triggers ("when I debuff someone"); cooldown-ready on charge restore; and `on_expire` (declared, never emitted — use `on_status_removed`). A reaction cannot cancel the lethal hit (reactions resolve a frame later). Item charges are spent when the item fires and refill with the next unit of the stack; they never regenerate over time the way ability charges do, and `ItemGenerator` rejects a `charge_restore_time` on items. An instant item (no cast point and no channel) fires without joining the command queue, so Shift does not queue it.
